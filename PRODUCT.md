# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Everyone explores freely: browse, search, and stream without registering. Authentication unlocks personal features (upload tracks, save to library, like songs). Creators use MelodyStream from home studios and laptops. Listeners discover and play music casually, like Spotify or SoundCloud.

## Product Purpose

A personal music streaming platform where creators upload audio, which is processed into HLS streams for high-quality adaptive playback. Success means creators feel proud sharing their work through a polished, distinctive interface, and listeners enjoy a seamless playback experience.

## Brand Personality

Bold, expressive, vibrant. The interface should feel like a stage, not a spreadsheet. Confident typography, warm energy, and a sense of creative momentum.

## Anti-references

- Not Spotify's cluttered homepage with endless content rows
- Not SoundCloud's busy, noisy feed
- Not a generic SaaS dashboard with neutral grays and safe choices
- Not the typical purple-blue "music app" palette that every streaming clone defaults to

## Design Principles

1. **Stage presence**: Every screen should feel intentional and composed, like a well-lit stage. No filler content, no throwaway sections.
2. **Warmth over coldness**: The interface should feel inviting and human, not sterile or corporate. Warm tones, not cold blues.
3. **Signal over noise**: Show what matters. A clean library beats a cluttered feed. One clear action beats five competing buttons.
4. **Creative confidence**: The UI should make creators feel good about their work. Premium presentation for every uploaded track.
5. **Rhythm in layout**: Vary spacing and emphasis to create visual rhythm, mirroring the musical context of the product.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.1 AA compliance
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion for animations
- Sufficient contrast ratios on dark backgrounds
- Keyboard-navigable player controls
